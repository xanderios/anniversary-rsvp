O castelo da Maria Alice

Thirteen scenes, from the loading screen to the family panel. Every picture of the garden, castle and hall is a real render of the low-poly scene modeled in Blender for this project. Buttons and labels are mock-ups of the interface, in Portuguese as guests will see it.

What changed since the first version
  • Fonts. The script and Garamond of your invitation card now run through the whole site, buttons included.
  • Smooth lighting. Soft bounced light and soft shadows. Towers, roofs and balloons are now smooth; trees, flowers and ground keep their facets. Palette untouched.
  • Clouds. Rebuilt as clusters of round spheres, large in the middle and small at the ends, like your reference.
  • Phone movement. New loading screen that asks permission to use the phone's motion (Cena 1).
  • The envelope. Seal drops, flap opens, the letter rises and unfolds in three. It is a working prototype you can play (Cena 8).
  • The invitation. Rebuilt in HTML over your own card art (Cena 9).
  • Answers. Yes or no only. No headcount. Changeable until 24 hours before the party.
  • Panel access. E-mail and password, created through an e-mailed link, two administrators at most.

Cena 9

O convite em HTML

How
The flowers, castle, ribbon and gold frame are your own artwork with the lettering lifted off. Every word on top is live text in matching fonts, with the crown, hearts and icons redrawn as vector shapes.
Why
Text stays razor sharp at any zoom, screen readers can read it aloud, and the address can be tapped to open the map.
Limit
The script font is a close match, not the identical one, so letter shapes in "Maria Alice" differ slightly from the original.

How it gets built

Plano de construção

  • 3D models. The Blender scene is exported as compact glTF files, with gates, flags and butterflies as separate moving pieces.
  • Soft light on phones. The smooth lighting in these frames is baked into the models in Blender, so a phone shows it without heavy real-time work.
  • In the browser. Three.js draws the scene. Scroll drives the camera, the gates, the glow and the fade. Mouse or phone tilt adds the small parallax.
  • Invitation. The HTML card and the envelope prototype on this page become the real component.
  • Server. A small Node server and one SQLite file on your VPS, with automatic HTTPS. HTTPS is also what lets iPhones grant the motion permission.
  • Domain. You register the name. I have not checked whether convite.ai or any other name is free.
  • E-mail. Account links and sign-in codes need a sending account (any SMTP provider). I need its details at deploy time.
  • Fallback. If a device cannot run 3D, or reduced motion is on, the journey plays as a crossfade of still renders.
